# BIST 0.2.0 — Imperial collaboration baseline

This release turns the research prototype into a repeatable collaborator build. It also aligns the main scientific path with the extended Hénon boundary map: stability is computed on (\mathbb{R}^2\times S^1), curvature has a quantitative wedge diagnostic, and thesis checkpoints have executable reports and an acceptance ledger.

For the Imperial visit, begin with `START_HERE.md` in the archive or the [collaborator package](./docs/imperial_collaborator_package.md) in the source tree. The reference Hénon and Duffing configurations are included. Every imported experiment is validated and recomputed locally.

The release should be interpreted as a reliable baseline for joint research, not as a claim of exhaustive orbit detection. The local extended-map calculations and reduced multipliers are reproduced. High-period normal-bundle membership and topological labels still require the recorded manifold/MIS/dual-repeller refinement tests. These limitations are stated in the tutorials, validation guide, and Wei target ledger.
